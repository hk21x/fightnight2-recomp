The first release of the unofficial native PC port of Fight Night Round 2 (original Xbox). It is not an emulator:
the game's own code is statically recompiled into a Windows program, with a new renderer, sound and input underneath.

**No game files are included.** You need your own copy of the game: the European Xbox release (PAL), as an `.iso` of
your disc or a folder with its files. The game is built on your PC from your disc.

## What's in it

- **True widescreen**, 21:9 and wider included, with the HUD and menus kept in proportion; the aspect changes as you
  fight. The menus keep their 16:9 shape with a blurred copy at the sides, and the arena's sides open out smoothly as
  a fight starts.
- **Smooth motion** (optional): the fights drawn at your frame rate, 144 or 240 frames a second, with the game
  moving at its own 50 a second.
- **Sharper graphics:** render scale up to 200%, MSAA up to 8x, FXAA or SMAA, sharpening, anisotropic filtering,
  enhanced world textures, and sharp text and HUD.
- **Mouse and keyboard:** Total Punch Control on the mouse, WASD to move, and the mouse in the menus.
- **Controllers:** Xbox, PlayStation and Switch pads with hot plugging and rumble, and modern button pictures.
- **PC settings:** F1 opens the settings window anywhere, and the main ones are in the game's own menus too: X on
  Options and X on the pause menu in a fight. Skip the intro movies, hide the HUD (F9), F12 screenshots, English,
  German or French.
- **Achievements:** 10, for the Career's wins, knockouts and belts, a created champ, the fight store and a retirement.
- **Unlock everything:** one setting makes every venue, boxer and item available; your save keeps its own progress.
- **Quit Game** on the title menu, and the menus' tab markers drawn as today's LT and RT.

## Getting started

1. Download **FightNight2Launcher.exe** below and put it in a folder of its own with a short path (for example
   `C:\Games\FightNight2`).
2. Run it. Windows may warn about an unrecognised app: **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Choose your disc image (or folder) and press **Get the game ready**. The launcher downloads the game's package
   (the program and a compiler, no game code) into its own folder, and your PC builds the game's code from your disc
   in about a minute. Nothing is installed in Windows. You can stop at any time; it carries on next time.
4. Press **Play**. In the game, **F1** opens the PC settings.

Requirements: Windows 10 or 11 (64 bit), a Direct3D 12 graphics card (Direct3D 11 also works) and about 400 MB
free besides your copy of the game.

## Known issues

- Tested on AMD and Intel Arc graphics; NVIDIA cards are expected to work but have not been tested yet.
- With smooth motion the boxers' limbs move at the game's 50 a second while the camera and the ring are smooth.
- The gym drills' pop-ups stay at 16:9.

Problems and questions: the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/PQjkKsdtc). Please include `launcher.log` (beside the
launcher) or the log in `FightNight2\play\logs`.
